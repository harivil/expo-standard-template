Copy to `docs/intent/<slug>.md`. This is the problem in the originator's own words, written **before**
anyone decides what to build. Whoever raised it owns this file; the spec that follows is someone
else's job.

Plain language is correct here. No technical framing is required, and inventing some usually loses
the detail that mattered.

## Problem

What cannot be done today, and who is blocked by it. Concrete beats general — how often it happens,
how long it takes, what people do instead.

## Proposed outcome

What should be true afterwards, from the user's side. Not a design, and not a screen — the change in
what someone can do.

## Affected users and systems

Who feels this, and which parts of the app or which services it touches.

## Constraints

What must stay true regardless of how it is built. Data that must not be stored or logged, an
existing flow that cannot change, a platform that must keep working, a deadline that is real.

## Open questions

What the originator does not know yet. An honest question here is cheaper than a wrong assumption in
the spec.
